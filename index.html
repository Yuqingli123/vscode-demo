AI Powered Programming

Project-Based Learning Course

Instructor Information

Instructor: LI Yuqing, High School Teacher

School: Meisha Honor Program

LI Yuqing is a dedicated high school teacher with a passion for integrating AI and technology into the classroom. With years of experience in education, LI Yuqing aims to inspire students to explore the world of AI and develop their skills through hands-on projects.

Course Description

This course introduces high school students to the exciting world of AI-powered programming. Through project-based learning, students will explore the fundamentals of artificial intelligence and machine learning, and apply their knowledge to create innovative projects.

Course Objectives

  • Understand the basics of AI and machine learning
  • Learn to program using AI tools and frameworks
  • Develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills
  • Create real-world projects using AI technologies

Course Structure

The course is divided into several modules, each focusing on a different aspect of AI-powered programming. Students will work on hands-on projects and collaborate with peers to enhance their learning experience.
